# Results (frozen protocol v5)

Generated by `summarize_v5.py` from `final_eval.json` and `eval_results.json` files. Detectors and policies are frozen (`12_frozen_protocol.md`); every number below is measured on the final-test partition or the final-test probe bank. Counts are exact. Separation is by invariant-feature groups and does not establish independence by host, capture session or time. The experiments are constrained flow-feature simulations.

**Intervals.** The final-test probe bank holds 13,569 flows but only 72 invariant-feature groups, and flows in a group are near-identical, so a plain binomial interval would be far too narrow. The 95% intervals in sections 1 and 2 resample whole groups (probe tables) or whole signatures (clean-traffic tables) with replacement (`intervals_v5.py`, 2,000 resamples). They describe sampling variation across groups for a fixed, already-trained detector; they do not include variation from retraining (see section 3 and Part B). An interval of [1.000, 1.000] means every resampled set of groups was fully detected.
