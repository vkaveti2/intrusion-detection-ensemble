# Fixed-payload comparison (frozen protocol v5)

Generated by `evaluate_fixed_payload.py`. A constrained flow-feature simulation: of the 1,250 actions only the five payload sizes change what the detectors see (flags are not simulated; delay only affects reward). The fixed policy sends one payload size at the smallest delay (0.1). The size is **chosen on the dev bank** (14 groups) and **evaluated on the final-test bank** (72 groups, 13,569 flows). Ties go to the smallest size. The DDQN agents train on the agent-train bank and are evaluated greedily on the final-test bank (2,000 probes per run). Counts are exact; reward for the DDQN agents is an estimate from its median delay.
